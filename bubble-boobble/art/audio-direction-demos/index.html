The Bubble Brigade

Three original audio directions · first composition and sound-design sketches

For choosing a direction. These are locally synthesized sketches, not final orchestral recordings or production game assets. No direction has been selected and game playback is unchanged. The three music arrangements share an original motif; their pace and instrumentation differ.

Compare the music first, then the effects at a comfortable volume. Each SFX reel plays: shot → pop → capture → food pickup → player downed → rescue, with silence between cues. All playback starts on your click. Starting another clip stops the previous one.
