# Original oblique Brigade dragon

Built 23 October 2026 with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool in identity-preserving edit mode. The only character pixels supplied to generation were the game's original `assets/cartoon/frames/dragon/green/idle-0.png` and an expanded canvas containing that same seed. No Cuphead character pixels were passed to generation or copied into these assets.

Reference study: local collection `97184-cuphead-overworld.md` and the actual down, diagonal-down, side, diagonal-up and up idle frames established how an elevated camera exposes the skull, shoulders and shoes. The theatrical cel adventure art guide pages 03 and 05 established dark contours, dominant readable silhouettes, simple flat local color, expressive hands and saturated actors against quieter world colors. The Brigade's established green, cream and yellow identity takes precedence over recoloring it to the guide's bottle-green swatch.

The original generated 24-pose sheet is `source/dragon-eightway-source-edit-v3.png`. A single sheet contains eight directional triplets: down, down-right, right, up-right, up, up-left, left, down-left. Each triplet holds idle, left-foot contact and right-foot contact. Fixed camera elevation is approximately 35 degrees. All poses are new drawings of the Brigade dragon, with no cup-headed character or borrowed map pixels.

Run `python3 tools/overworld/build-oblique-dragon.py`. This extracts the 24 connected silhouettes, repacks them without changing their art, invokes the preserved sprite-pipeline normalizer once across the full strip with one global scale, then pads each to 128×128 at the shared foot anchor `[64,120]`. It derives all ten existing skin palettes using the same established green-channel recoloring rule. Runtime assets live at `assets/overworld-shared/dragon-oblique/{skin}/{direction}-{idle|walk-0|walk-1}.png`. The preview and exact bounds are under `source/oblique-normalized/`.

The renderer holds idle still. While moving it alternates idle / contact 0 / idle / contact 1, with eight facing directions retained when movement stops. Guide and vendor use matching oblique blue and orange idle poses. Source frames have real transparency; exported RGB under alpha zero is cleared. This art has been inspected as a contact sheet; actual engine animation and scene scale are separate verification steps.

Prompt specification, first edit: preserve the shipped slender friendly green dragon, cream muzzle/belly, pink cheeks, black oval eyes, rounded yellow dorsal spines, mitten hands, rounded cream shoes and curved tail. Draw 24 complete isolated sprites on a transparent 1536×1024 sheet, 6 columns by 4 rows, each direction's idle/left-contact/right-contact grouped in order. Use a fixed downward 35-degree oblique camera, consistent sizes and foot baselines, strong hand-inked vintage cartoon contours and flat muted colors. No horns, wings, glossy shading, labels, grid, ground, shadow, scenery or Cuphead character.

Targeted second edit: preserve the exact 24-pose identity and grid; raise the fixed camera to show skull and shoulder tops, foreshortened torso/legs and shoe tops. Require clear transparent cutouts, opaque character interiors and no glow or haze. The underlying image's background alpha was verified zero; PNG exports clear otherwise invisible RGB data outside the cutouts.

Third targeted edit replaced every idle with both shoes planted and emphasized alternating opposite arms/legs in contact poses. Direct front/back generated contacts retained a screen-side bias; the exporter derives their second contact by horizontally mirroring the first contact. This preserves symmetric front/back identity while producing a true opposite-foot contact. The six other directional pairs retain their separately authored contact drawings. No runtime idle animation is added.
