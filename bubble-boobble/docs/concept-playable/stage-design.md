# Five-tier cartoon stage

This milestone changes only the explicitly selected `cartoon-courtyard` arena and its presentation. Original campaign tiles, original courtyard factories, controls and movement balance remain intact.

The board is 60×24 tiles. The initial 44×16 trial was rejected after its actual wide capture put the crown at 356px; the taller stage moves that ledge up substantially while retaining safe jump clearance. Five playable tiers, including the floor, have collision tops at rows 3, 8, 13, 18 and 23. Every adjacent tier is 120 world pixels apart; the unchanged unupgraded jump rises 126 world pixels. Four curated upper patterns retain campaign variation. Broad shelves overlap across two continuous ascent lanes at columns 12 and 30, with additional staggered cross-stage routes. Ten authored player spawns place two players on each tier while keeping P 1 on the floor. The cartoon-only grounded enemy scan also includes the crown; original arenas retain their row 4 scan start. Enemy count, family and level reward/element metadata are copied from each original authored round.

The engine tests exercise actual unupgraded `stepGame` movement from the real floor spawn: walk to each lane, jump four times, and land exactly 23→18→13→8→3 without repositioning between jumps. Both lanes pass in all four patterns. Actual diagonal 120px transfers, cross-gap jumps and missed-jump recovery also pass without balance changes. A separate graph check verifies safe 44px standing bodies and connectivity from every player spawn. Evidence is in `candidate-controls.json` (the earlier 44×16 result remains clearly intermediate in `focused-controls-initial.json`).

Camera ownership remains with the existing WorldView-driven flat2d engine camera. The board renderer shares the HUD's presentation-only reserve helper: 48 logical pixels above play, 64 below on every screen. The must-see envelope includes 140 world pixels above row zero: the crown jump and identity badge can reach 130 pixels above zero, leaving 10 pixels of additional clearance. Eight extra pixels below the physical board keep the thicker floor art above the footer. The background bleeds across the full visible canvas.

Platform art extends 32 pixels down from the exact collision top, giving thicker cream caps. The visible wall is a 24 pixel inner masonry strip at the existing collision boundary; its outer backing tile stays solid in simulation while the painted background continues behind it. Player art and collision envelopes are unchanged. The HELP plaque is presentation-only ink/cream geometry above downed actors, with a small pointer; its text is supplied by the shared presentation text pool.

Final actual wide/native/tall/ultrawide captures, render counts and authored framing bounds are recorded in `composition-captures.json` beside their PNGs. Bounds in that file are explicitly labeled as calculations from the layout contract; the images and camera/render snapshots are actual engine output.

At 1536×864, the final 64px-footer contract puts the crown collision top at 271.24px and the floor collision top at 754.95px. The initial 44×16 comparison placed them at 355.99px and 761.36px. The final crown moves 84.75px upward; the stage occupies 94.48% of the viewport width. The full highest-jump badge envelope clears the header by 10.08 physical pixels; the floor art ends at the footer boundary. These are authored bound calculations accompanying actual engine images, not estimates from a resized mockup. All four actual combat captures remain below 14 draws: wide 8, native 11, tall 11, ultrawide 8. Actual HELP/start/boss frames report 9/9/10 draws.
